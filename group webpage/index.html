Our Mission Statement

Our team is building a high-efficiency FastAPI backend to optimize restaurant operations and make guest checkouts seamless. We combine data-driven engineering with clear Agile workflows to deliver high-quality, reliable code on schedule.

Team Working Rules

  • Commitment: Every developer must log task milestones on our board and submit verified, passing commits.
  • Courage: Flag technical blockages or timeline delays inside the team chat immediately for open group support.
  • Focus: Restrict development strictly to active sprint backlog tasks, avoiding unnecessary feature additions.
  • Respect: Honor external work windows and course limits by utilizing structured, asynchronous text statuses.

Sprint Cadence

Our team holds weekly sync sessions over Discord/Zoom on Tuesdays at 5:30 PM to evaluate milestone progress, clear roadblocks, and distribute upcoming sprint features.
